How to Play Rock, Paper, Scissors

Rock, Paper, Scissors is a simple game for two players where both people pick one of three choices—rock, paper, or scissors—at the same time. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, and paper beats rock. If both players pick the same thing, it’s a tie. Each time someone wins a round, they get 1 point, and the goal is to reach a set number of points or just play a certain number of rounds. To reset the game, you just set both players’ scores back to zero and clear their choices so you can start fresh.
